About DEMO VEGGIES

Dedicated to providing fresh, carefully graded vegetables with reliable wholesale supply and friendly retail service.

Our Business

Committed to Produce Freshness & Customer Satisfaction

DEMO VEGGIES is a wholesale and retail vegetable business specializing in everyday kitchen staples — primarily fresh tomatoes, whole coconuts, and fresh garlic.

Our business was established with a straightforward operational philosophy: provide customers with fresh, dependable vegetable stock without unnecessary complications. We understand that whether a customer is purchasing a small quantity for home cooking or multiple crates for a commercial food business, quality and freshness are non-negotiable.

We work closely with direct farm sourcing channels to bring vegetables straight to our distribution point. Every consignment that arrives is inspected, hand-graded, and sorted to maintain high standards of firmness, cleanliness, and shelf stability.

Daily Operations

How We Handle Your Produce

  • 1
    Careful Intake & Inspection Incoming consignments are checked immediately for freshness, physical condition, and moisture integrity.
  • 2
